Why Your Body Needs Exercise

Your body is like a supercar the more you use it, the stronger it gets! Kids who move for 60 minutes every day are happeir, sleep better, and do better at school.

Dancing, swimming, football, yoga andything that gets your heart beating faster counts. Keep moving and have fun!

quick Health Tips

  • Sleep 9 to 11 hours every night
